Cosmetic Dentistry

Cosmetic dentistry corrects the imperfections that hold your smile back, from discoloration and chips to gaps and uneven proportions. Through personalized treatments such as teeth whitening, veneers, and bonding, we help you achieve a refined, natural smile that restores both function and confidence in your everyday presentation.

  • Corrects discoloration, chips, gaps, and uneven or aged-looking teeth.
  • Delivers results designed to look natural and complement your features.
  • Strengthens confidence in professional, social, and personal settings.

Dental Implants

Dental implants replace missing or damaged teeth with a permanent-feeling foundation that supports crowns, bridges, or dentures. Using titanium tooth roots surgically placed as part of a structured treatment plan, we help restore your ability to eat, speak, and smile without hesitation while protecting your long-term oral health.

  • Provides a durable, stable replacement for missing teeth.
  • Helps prevent further bone loss and preserves facial structure.
  • Restores natural chewing function and speech clarity.

Restorative Dentistry

Restorative dentistry repairs damaged, worn, or compromised teeth to restore comfort and function. Whether addressing structural damage or the effects of daily wear, our approach combines precision and care to help your smile perform the way it should, without disrupting your long-term oral health.

  • Repairs damaged or worn teeth to restore comfort and function.
  • Addresses underlying issues before they affect surrounding teeth.
  • Supports long-term oral health alongside a natural-looking result.

MEET

Dr. Marisol Ashford

Dentist

With more than 37 years in dentistry and a passion for facial aesthetics, Dr. Marisol Ashford brings together clinical expertise, artistic vision, and personalized care. A 1989 graduate of the Regional University College of Dental Medicine, she has spent her career pursuing excellence in cosmetic and restorative dentistry, and her appreciation for facial harmony led her into facial aesthetics, where she has refined her work with neuromodulators and dermal fillers for more than 14 years. Her philosophy is simple: the most beautiful results enhance, rather than change, what makes each person uniquely beautiful. Drawing on her background in dentistry and anesthesia, she offers specialized techniques including dental nerve blocks and nitrous oxide to keep aesthetic treatments as comfortable and virtually painless as possible.
