Frequently Asked Questions

What is Micropen EVO Microneedling?

Micropen EVO is an FDA-cleared microneedling device that creates controlled micro-injuries in the skin to trigger your body's natural collagen and elastin production. It is used to address fine lines, acne scars, enlarged pores, uneven texture, and stretch marks. Candidacy is assessed at your consultation.

Is Micropen EVO Microneedling painful?

Most patients describe mild discomfort. Topical numbing is applied beforehand to support comfort throughout your session. Individual experience varies.

How many sessions will I need?

Most patients may benefit from 3 to 6 sessions spaced 4 weeks apart, depending on skin concerns and goals. Your physician-led clinical team will advise at your consultation.

Is there downtime?

Minimal. Mild redness similar to a light sunburn may occur for 1 to 3 days. Most patients return to normal activities immediately. Individual recovery varies.

Is Micropen EVO appropriate for my skin type?

Micropen EVO may be appropriate across a wide range of skin types and tones. Candidacy is assessed at your consultation by our physician-led clinical team.

Can Micropen EVO be combined with other treatments?

Yes, in appropriate cases. Your physician-led clinical team will advise on whether a combination approach suits your goals and medical history.
