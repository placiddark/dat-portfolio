Your dermal filler questions, answered

How long do dermal fillers last?

Most fillers last 6 to 12 months, or sometimes longer, depending on the product, treated area, and your metabolism.

Are dermal fillers safe?

Yes, when administered by trained clinicians like our cosmetic injection specialists using best-in-class, FDA-approved products and appropriate technique.

Will I look overdone?

Our physician-led philosophy guarantees we prioritize subtle refinement and balance. We aim for harmony and proportion specific to your facial structure, ensuring a refreshed look, not volume for volume’s sake.

How soon will I see results, and is there downtime?

Results are visible right away, and refine as minor swelling settles. Downtime is typically minimal, allowing you to quickly resume your routine.

How are fillers different from neurotoxins?

Fillers restore lost volume and enhance facial shape. Neurotoxins (like Botox) relax the muscles that create dynamic expression lines.
